# Project Description


Our senior design project will be a vinyl record album library/tracker. There will be three main parts. 
1. Vinyl collection tracking app. This will be somewhere you can keep track of your vinyl collection and see insights into its value over time. 
2. Play tracking, the first step will be NFC chips that you tap before you play it, and it logs a play. 
3. Real-time play tracking which will take the output data from the record, identify the song, and track playtime in real time like a Spotify tracker.
